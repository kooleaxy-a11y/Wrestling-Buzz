In December 2001, WWE held a tournament at Vengeance to determine the first Undisputed WWF Champion. Chris Jericho defeated The Rock and Stone Cold Steve Austin on the same night. Which superstar did Jericho defeat in the final match to become the Undisputed Champion?
A. Kurt Angle
B. The Rock
C. Stone Cold Steve Austin
D. The Undertaker
✅ Correct Answer: C. Stone Cold Steve Austin

At WrestleMania 21 in 2005, WWE introduced the first Money in the Bank ladder match. Six superstars competed for a briefcase containing a contract for a championship opportunity. Which superstar won the match?
A. Chris Jericho
B. Edge
C. Shelton Benjamin
D. Christian
✅ Correct Answer: B. Edge

At WrestleMania XXX in 2014, The Undertaker suffered his first defeat at WrestleMania after winning 21 consecutive matches at the event. Which superstar defeated him, ending the legendary streak?
A. Triple H
B. Randy Orton
C. Brock Lesnar
D. Roman Reigns
✅ Correct Answer: C. Brock Lesnar

At Survivor Series 2002, six superstars competed in the first-ever Elimination Chamber match for the World Heavyweight Championship. The match featured Triple H, Shawn Michaels, Chris Jericho, Booker T, Kane and which other superstar?
A. Rob Van Dam
B. The Undertaker
C. Kurt Angle
