Latest from the Buzz team

1. Money in the Bank fallout

Penta is Mr. Money in the Bank

At the 10 October event, Penta beat CM Punk, Kevin Owens, Trick Williams, Je'Von Evans and Bron Breakker to win the men's contract. It gives him a future shot at a world title. WWE hasn't said when he'll cash in.

Tiffany Stratton wins the women's briefcase

She beat Jacy Jayne, Lash Legend, Roxanne Perez, Sol Ruca and Lola Vice. It's her second Money in the Bank win, so fans are already asking when she'll use it.

Both briefcases build suspense around surprise title challenges. Any cash-in target or date is speculation until WWE announces it.

2. The Bloodline, The Usos and The 946

This is one of the main stories on Raw. Roman Reigns and The Usos are now in the fight against The 946. Solo Sikoa formed an uneasy alliance with The Usos, but their match against The 946 ended in a no-contest on 12 October when Carmelo Hayes returned and interfered.

WWE's recap says Hayes then helped Royce Keys, Bronco Nima and Lucien Price attack Sikoa and The Usos. The big questions: is Hayes officially with The 946, and how will Reigns, Sikoa and The Usos respond?

3. Reigns retains, Penta's party gets interrupted

At Money in the Bank, World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns beat LA Knight. The 946 showed up after the match and it turned into a brawl with The Usos and Solo Sikoa.

On the next Raw, Penta celebrated his contract win. Bron Breakker then hit an already-injured Je'Von Evans with a Spear, which keeps Breakker in the spotlight and adds tension around Penta and Evans.

4. Women's division: Liv Morgan vs Chelsea Green

Liv Morgan won the Women's World Championship in a Triple Threat against champion Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch. On Raw, WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green confronted Morgan ahead of a champion-versus-champion match at Crown Jewel.

Roxanne Perez also beat Chelsea Green in a non-title match on Raw, helped by distractions involving Morgan and Raquel Rodriguez. That gives Perez momentum.

5. Oba Femi turns down Paul Heyman

After beating Bronson Reed at Money in the Bank, Oba Femi rejected Paul Heyman's offer to join his circle on Raw. Femi looks set on choosing his own path. Whether Heyman tries again hasn't been confirmed.

6. Other results worth knowing

  • Chad Gable kept the Intercontinental Championship by beating AAA World Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fenix on Raw.
  • Rey Mysterio beat Big Cass with help from Dragon Lee.
  • Roxanne Perez beat Chelsea Green in a non-title match.
  • Oba Femi beat Bronson Reed at Money in the Bank despite interference and outside distractions.
